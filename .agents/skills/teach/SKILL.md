# Teach

Teach a concept when Leo explicitly asks for a lesson or explanation. Follow the level, format, and focus he names. If he gives no format preference, answer the question directly and add background or an example only when it makes the idea clearer.

Explain the relevant ideas in a connected order, define unfamiliar terms when they first matter, and distinguish established facts from assumptions or open questions. Use a worked example when it makes the concept easier to apply. For current or version-dependent details, consult an authoritative source and link it.

Teaching supports Leo's decisions; it does not make them. When a research or logic choice is at issue, explain applicable options and tradeoffs, then let Leo choose. Never test his competence, require a demonstration of understanding, or withhold ordinary execution because he has not shown that understanding. Do not turn experience into lasting preferences, a new skill, or an instruction unless Leo explicitly asks.

At specification approval or when a new substantive choice appears, briefly offer to explain a relevant method when it may be unfamiliar. Routine operations such as calculating a correlation need no teaching prompt.

Do not implement a research change or create a design document as part of a lesson unless Leo separately requests that work.

**Done:** Leo receives an explanation at the requested depth, with uncertainty and sources clear where they matter.
