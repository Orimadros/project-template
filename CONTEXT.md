# Research template

Shared vocabulary for the reusable research infrastructure and the projects that adopt it.

## Language

**Template**: The reusable starting point for Leo's research repositories. Its purpose is to improve productivity, organization, research quality, and reproducibility across projects.

**Research project**: An individual research effort with its own question, evidence, and outputs. Multiple research projects already use the template and have their own evolving needs.

**Mentat**: An agent that helps Leo identify decisions, understand methods, weigh alternatives, and implement his choices. Leo retains authority over all research and logic decisions.

**Change contract**: An agreed description of a change to the repository, which may affect several files. It is archived after acceptance; a later change receives a new contract.

**Canonical dataset**: A designated, assembled dataset from which subsequent analysis draws data at an agreed observation level. Its contents and construction choices are approved by the researcher.

**Pending approval**: A proposed change whose code and generated outputs are available together for Leo's inspection but have not yet been incorporated into the accepted project.
